# Indian Railways Dataset (Delhi NCR & Uttar Pradesh)

## Scope
Verified major Indian Railways stations and connecting corridors across Delhi NCR and Uttar Pradesh.

## Stations Covered
- **Delhi Terminals**: New Delhi (NDLS), Old Delhi (DLI), Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM), Anand Vihar Terminal (ANVT), Delhi Sarai Rohilla (DEE)
- **NCR Junctions**: Ghaziabad (GZB), Meerut City (MTC), Meerut Cantt (MUT)
- **Uttar Pradesh Major Stations**: Aligarh (ALJN), Tundla (TDL), Agra Cantt (AGC), Agra Fort (AF), Mathura (MTJ), Moradabad (MB), Bareilly (BE), Kanpur Central (CNB), Lucknow Charbagh (LKO/LJN), Ayodhya (AY/AYC), Prayagraj (PRYJ), Varanasi (BSB), Gorakhpur (GKP)

## Provenance
- Official National Train Enquiry System (NTES) & Indian Railways: https://www.indianrailways.gov.in
- Timetable data verified against official IRCTC / NTES published schedules.
- No synthetic edges: all connections represent verified scheduled train operations.
- Status: `verified`
