# Namo Bharat (RRTS) Datasets

## Scope
Verified operational and priority corridor stations and connections for the **Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut RRTS Corridor** operated by NCRTC (National Capital Region Transport Corporation).

## Stations Covered
- **Sarai Kale Khan** (Delhi mult-modal interchange with Hazrat Nizamuddin Railway Station and Pink Line)
- **New Ashok Nagar** (Delhi interchange with Blue Line)
- **Anand Vihar** (Major multi-modal hub with Anand Vihar Terminal Railway Station, Blue/Pink Lines, and ISBT)
- **Sahibabad** (Ghaziabad)
- **Ghaziabad** (Interchange with Ghaziabad Junction railway & Red line connector)
- **Guldhar**
- **Duhai** (Operational junction / Depot branch)
- **Muradnagar**
- **Modi Nagar South**
- **Modi Nagar North**
- **Meerut South** (Bhudbaral, operational terminus in Meerut)
